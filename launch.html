Field to FHIR

Closing the loop between EMS field impressions and hospital discharge dispositions

Field to FHIR helps EMS professionals see what happened to their patients after hospital handoff by bringing the EMS field impression, crew and vital signs together with the hospital's diagnosis and outcome in one view.

The crew records a field impression and vital signs on scene. Each crew member's licensure level is part of the record.

The hospital visit links back to the EMS impression. The hospital records the final diagnosis, vital signs and discharge outcome.

Field to FHIR shows both side by side, so the crew can see whether their impression matched the hospital's diagnosis.

EMS providers sign in through SMART App Launch 2.2.0. This app only reads data. It never changes it.
