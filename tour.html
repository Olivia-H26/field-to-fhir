Mission

Field to FHIR helps EMS professionals see what happened to their patients after hospital handoff by bringing the EMS field impression, crew and vital signs together with the hospital's diagnosis and outcome in one view.

Vision

I'd like the app to compare each EMS field impression with the hospital's final diagnosis so an EMS crew can learn whether their impression was right and improve their care.

What the comparison shows

The dashboard compares the EMS and hospital diagnosis codes.
